# Legal and Compliance Questions

This document records questions for validation. It is not legal advice.

## General

- What consumer-protection obligations apply to a verification service?
- What representations can the service make about evidence and risk?
- What disclaimers and limitations are required?
- What personal-data processing is necessary?
- What data sources may be accessed and under what conditions?
- What retention period is appropriate for evidence?

## TC-01A — Property

- What information may be collected about counterparties?
- What verification claims can be made regarding ownership, authority or identity?
- Which documents can be processed?
- What liability could arise from an incorrect verification result?

## TC-01B — Seasonal Rental

- What consumer-protection rules apply to the relevant rental relationship?
- What information may be collected about landlords, intermediaries and properties?
- What evidence can be used to verify existence and offer consistency?
- What platform terms restrict automated collection or reuse of information?
- What claims can be made regarding fraud or risk?

## Privacy

Privacy requirements must be treated as an architecture concern, not added after implementation.

Before processing personal information in production, establish:

- purpose;
- lawful basis;
- minimization;
- retention;
- access controls;
- user rights;
- evidence provenance.
